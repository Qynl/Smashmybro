# GETAWAY — Round 3: living frontier pass

Round 2 made jobs and hideouts matter. Round 3 makes the county itself feel like a place that is moving around the player.

## Incidents

- Seed a small set of authored-feeling incidents into each county: train crossing, broken-down wagon, washed-out bridge, rival riders, and sheriff search.
- Incidents should be readable and avoid becoming unavoidable punishment.
- An incident should change a route or create a story beat, not simply spawn an enemy on top of the player.

## Route pressure

- Show incident markers on the world and minimap.
- Let the player choose between a fast road through trouble and a slower back route.
- Make sheriff search rings and incidents use the same visual language.

## Audio feedback

- Add a low, quiet engine tone tied to speed and vehicle type.
- Add a storm/wind bed and a short impact transient.
- Keep audio synthesized and dependency-free so the GitHub Pages build stays static.

## Frontier geography

- Add mesa silhouettes, canyon walls, river banks, rail structures, water towers, and county-line geometry.
- Use landmarks as navigation tools rather than filling the whole map with generic buildings.
- Maintain the low-resolution stylized western look.

## Quality bar

A player should be able to describe the route they took using the county itself: “I went around the train, crossed Blackwater, hid at the livery, and came out behind the mesa.”
