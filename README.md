# GETAWAY // Dustfall County

A stylized retro-western outlaw escape sandbox for the browser. Drive procedural frontier counties, pull off high-stakes multi-stage bank heists and train ambushes, outrun the sheriff posse with spike caltrops and drifting, and tune your getaway vehicles in safehouse garages.

**Play:** open `index.html` directly or serve this folder with any static web server (`python3 -m http.server 8000`).

---

## Controls

- **Drive / Steer:** `WASD` or `Arrow Keys` (supports `ZQSD` French layouts)
- **Handbrake / Drift:** `Space` (accumulates drift combo bonus & leaves tire skid marks)
- **Nitro Boost:** `Shift` (supercharges acceleration with exhaust flame particles)
- **Interact / Action:** `E`, `F`, or `Enter` (enter/exit vehicles, take contracts, open safehouse garages)
- **Spike Caltrops Trap:** `C` or `G` (drops spike strips behind your wagon to blow out pursuers' tires)
- **Horn / Sheriff Siren:** `H` or `R`
- **Pause Menu:** `Escape`

---

## Key Systems & Features

### 1. Polished Physics & Guaranteed Unblocked Movement
- Smooth axis-separated collision sliding along walls, timber boardwalks, and buildings.
- Automatic de-penetration safety preventing vehicles or pedestrians from ever getting stuck.
- Dedicated open courtyards and driveways for all safehouse hideouts and job telegraph offices.
- Low-speed steering and intuitive reverse maneuvering.

### 2. Multi-Stage Outlaw Contracts
- **The Great County Bank Vault Heist:** Break into the bank vault, secure the cash bags, and escape a 3+ star sheriff pursuit across county lines.
- **Railroad Payroll Ambush:** Intercept the moving procedural freight train at the rail crossing and snatch the loot.
- **Moonshine Express:** Fragile cargo run across rough canyon trails where violent collisions damage payout.
- **Witness & Partner Extraction:** Rescue an outlaw partner under sheriff surveillance and rush them to safehouse hideouts.
- **Burn & Switch:** Ditch hot wanted vehicles, torch them, and switch to clean civilian transport.

### 3. Safehouse Outlaw Garages & Vehicle Tuning
- Pull into any of the 3 Safehouse Garages (**The Switchyard**, **Cinder Hotel & Livery**, **Blackwater Barn**) to access the workshop:
  - **Repair & Respray:** Instantly restores wagon condition and wipes all wanted heat.
  - **V8 Engine Tuning:** Stage 1 & 2 upgrades boosting top speed and acceleration.
  - **Reinforced Steel Ram-Bumper:** Increases durability and smashes sheriff cruisers.
  - **Rally Mud/Drift Tires:** Maximum traction in rainstorms and sand trails.
  - **Smuggler's Floorboards:** Secret hollowed compartments granting +50% contract rewards.
  - **Caltrop Ammo Refill:** Restock deployable tire-shredding spike traps.
  - **Vehicle Fleet Dealership:** Unlock and switch between 7 vehicles (Dustwind Coupe, Iron Horse V8 Muscle, Mule Runner, Silver Bullet Roadster, Armored War Wagon, Stage Taxi, Sheriff Interceptor).

### 4. Interactive Living World & Sound
- **Active Freight Train:** Locomotive engine with glowing headlamps, coal tender, cargo cars, and caboose traveling on scheduled routes with crossing whistles.
- **Destructible Props:** Smash wooden fences, crates, water barrels, and cacti for rampage cash bonuses.
- **Dynamic 5-Tier Law Posse:** Visual sight cones, last-known search zones on minimap, and tactical roadblocks.
- **Web Audio API Procedural Sound Suite:** Dynamic engine pitch rumble, tire drift screeching, wood and metal crash crunching, steam train whistles, gunshots, lightning thunder, and western acoustic guitar licks.
